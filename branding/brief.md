# Lightair · Brand Brief

## Essence

**Light + air.** Lightair is the calm studio where a creator's show goes on air. The brand is **flat, simple and confident**: one idea per mark, drawn with a few solid shapes. It should feel like a well-made tool (Linear, Things, Panic's apps), never like generated art.

| Attribute | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Form | Flat, geometric, a few solid shapes | Glow, glass, 3-D, gradients, sparkles, texture |
| Tone | Calm, confident, quiet | Loud, futuristic, "AI" |
| Colour | Off-black, white, one accent | Rainbow, gradients, neon |
| Detail | Works as a one-colour silhouette | Depends on effects to read |

## Colour

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Ink (dark) | `#141414` |
| Paper (light) | `#F5F5F7` / white |
| Accent | `#5B6CFF` (solid blue-violet) |
| On-air dot (tiny, sparingly) | `#FF3B4A` |

## App icon

- macOS rounded-square (squircle) with standard margins on a 1024×1024 canvas, transparent outside.
- **Flat**: solid fills only. No gradients, shadows, highlights, glow or glass.
- At most two colours plus one small accent.
- One simple mark that reads at 16 px and still works in one colour.

### Concepts (flat)

| Concept | Idea |
|---|---|
| **E · Tally** | A solid red dot, the on-air light, in a minimal frame |
| **F · Horizon** | A half circle of light rising over lines of air |
| **G · L-dot** | A bold geometric L with a red on-air dot in its corner |
| **H · Breeze** | A solid circle (light) trailing three rounded lines (air) |

## Wordmark

- The word **Lightair**, flat, one solid colour, set in a clean sans serif.
- Optional single detail: the dot of an i in the accent colour. Nothing else.
- Versions: ink on light, white on dark, and a lockup with the icon.
- Final wordmark and icon are rebuilt as **vector SVG** from the chosen concept.
