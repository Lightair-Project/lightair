# Platform logos

From Simple Icons 13.21.0 (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0), unchanged:
youtube, youtubeshorts, twitch, facebook, kick, tiktok, instagram, x, linkedin, bilibili,
niconico, xiaohongshu, rumble.
They identify the streaming destinations in the Outputs panel and stay subject to each
brand's own guidelines. Platforms Simple Icons doesn't have (Restream, CHZZK, SOOP, TwitCasting, Huya, DouYu,
WeChat Channels, 17LIVE, Mirrativ, PandaTV, Whowatch, CIME) use a text mark on their colour.
